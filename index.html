Methodology & limitations

Transparent definitions

Interview reach: candidates with interview milestone or later stage / all selected candidates.

Interview → offer: offer evidence / interview evidence. Open cases remain in the denominator.

Offer → hire: hires / offer evidence. This is a proxy, not a verified offer acceptance rate.

Scheduling lead time: calendar days between invitation and interview; not administrative scheduling turnaround.

Overdue: follow-up due before May 31, 2027, not completed.

Stage age: synthetic snapshot estimate, not reconstructed historical transitions.

Limitations: no real ATS, no actual candidate data, no statistical churn or fairness model. Synthetic milestone records may be incomplete; conversion is not cohort-matured.
